How the adaptive test works

Module 1 decides your Module 2

The digital SAT has two sections: Reading and Writing, then Math. Each section has two modules. How you do on Module 1 decides whether you get the easier or the harder Module 2, and only the harder one leads to the top scores. Our practice tests work the same way.

Frequently asked questions

Are these official SAT questions?

No. Every question here is written by our team, or generated by AI when you ask for it, in the official digital SAT format. For official questions, use the College Board’s free Bluebook app and SAT Suite Question Bank. We recommend using both.

How accurate is the score estimate?

Treat it as a ballpark. The real SAT scores each question with item response theory. Our estimate uses how many you got right and which Module 2 you reached, which is good for tracking progress but is not a guarantee.

Where is my progress saved?

If you sign in with Google, your progress is saved to your account and follows you to any device. Without an account, it stays in this browser on this device, and clearing your browser data starts fresh.

Can the AI tutor make mistakes?

Sometimes. It sees our answer key and explanation, but AI can still get things wrong. If an explanation doesn’t make sense, ask it to check again or message us.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Everything works on phones. For full practice tests, a laptop feels closer to test day.

Is this the same as Bluebook?

It follows the same structure and tools: timer, question navigator, mark for review, answer eliminator, annotation, calculator and reference sheet. It is our own practice site and is not affiliated with the College Board.
