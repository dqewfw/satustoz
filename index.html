Although the Aral Sea once supported a thriving fishing industry, decades of water diversion caused it to shrink. Recent efforts to restore its northern section have been ______: fish populations there have recovered enough that local fishing has resumed.

Which choice completes the text with the most logical and precise word or phrase?

B is right. The colon promises evidence: fish came back and fishing resumed, so the efforts have been encouraging. “Negligible” and “disastrous” contradict that evidence, and nothing says the result was “inevitable”.

The function f is defined by f(x) = 2x² − 18. What is the positive value of x for which f(x) = 0?

A is right. Set 2x² − 18 = 0, so 2x² = 18 and x² = 9. The solutions are 3 and −3, and the positive one is 3. Choice C is the value of x², not x.

How accurate is the score estimate?

Treat it as a ballpark. The real SAT scores each question with item response theory. Our estimate uses how many you got right and which Module 2 you reached, which is good for tracking progress but is not a guarantee.
